# BD Target Categories — Web3/Crypto

Generic categories to prioritize by speed-to-close and mission alignment. Substitute your org's actual products where noted. Read the full entry before initiating outreach on any target category.

---

## Tier 1 — Fastest to close, highest alignment

### Protocol teams with an acute, current need
**Examples:** A minority-client or minority-implementation team facing a diversity/adoption risk, a protocol mid-upgrade needing operator education, a project pre-launch needing distribution.
**Why now:** They have a live, time-boxed need. Forced-action moments (upgrades, migrations, launches) create urgency that shortens sales cycles.
**What they want:** Reach into audiences/regions they can't access alone; credibility with the community.
**What to sell:** Sponsored education/awareness content in your owned channels.
**Deal structure:** Sponsored content (fixed fee per edition/campaign) + optional co-production.
**Contact:** X/Discord DMs — warm first, be present in their community before outreach. Technical leads, DevRel, or ecosystem roles over founders.
**Expected deal size:** Small-to-mid, often recurring across similar future events (e.g. upgrade cycles).

---

### Staking / validator infrastructure
**Examples:** Staking protocols, liquid staking providers, validator services.
**Why now:** They need operator/validator education and regional growth. Upgrade or migration cycles create forced-action content moments.
**What they want:** Operator education, regional growth, brand presence in builder communities.
**What to sell:** Newsletter/content sponsorship + featured listings, if your org has them.
**Deal structure:** Recurring sponsorship tied to upgrade cycles + optional listing.
**Contact:** DevRel, growth, or ecosystem teams. Contributor/community programs are good entry points for tightly-knit communities — warm intro preferred.

---

### Node infrastructure / dev tooling companies
**Examples:** RPC providers, node hosting, dev tooling, monitoring services.
**Why now:** Upgrade cycles and migrations are their highest-demand moments — operators are actively searching for tooling right then.
**What they want:** Reach to operators/developers globally during high-intent windows.
**What to sell:** Sponsored slot in timely educational content.
**Deal structure:** Fixed sponsorship per edition or campaign. Commercial — direct fee conversation is fine.
**Contact:** Email or LinkedIn fine for commercial companies.

---

### Hackathon / community event platforms
**Examples:** Regional or global hackathon organizers, builder community platforms.
**Why now:** Need regional distribution to grow applicant pools outside their core markets.
**What they want:** Quality applicants in new markets, regional credibility.
**What to sell:** Event listings + regional amplification + co-branded campaigns.
**Deal structure:** Per-event listing fee + optional co-promotion package.
**Contact:** Partnership or growth teams — many have a formal partnerships process.

---

## Tier 2 — Larger deals, longer cycle

### L1/L2 protocols
**Why now:** Many chains compete for the same builder pool and need differentiated reach.
**What they want:** Developer adoption outside their core markets, credibility with serious builders, community partnerships.
**What to sell:** Featured listings + regional spotlights + grant/hackathon co-funding, if applicable.
**Deal structure:** Package deals preferred (listing + placement + optional co-branded program).
**Contact:** Ecosystem/DevRel teams, not VC-facing BD — find the people running developer programs.

---

### Institutional / TradFi crypto entrants
**Examples:** Traditional finance institutions moving into tokenization, custody, or on-chain products.
**Why now:** Moving from exploration to execution. Need ecosystem credibility and community relationships.
**What they want:** Ecosystem legitimacy, builder community relationships, talent pipeline access, market intelligence.
**What to sell:** Co-branded research using your data, curated talent pipelines, editorial presence.
**Deal structure:** Longer-term partnerships, formal proposal required.
**Contact:** Email to digital assets / blockchain strategy teams. LinkedIn appropriate. Warm intros significantly help.
**Expected cycle:** Longest (3–6 months), typically highest value.

---

### University / academic Web3 programs
**Why now:** Producing the next wave of developers, need legitimate ecosystem pathways for students.
**What they want:** Real opportunities for students, credibility for their programs, ecosystem connections.
**What to sell:** Formal distribution channel for student opportunities; partner listing for the program.
**Deal structure:** Institutional partnership — often non-revenue initially, with co-funding potential later.
**Contact:** Faculty advisors or student club leads (often active on X/Discord).

---

## Tier 3 — Mission-aligned, non-revenue

### Public goods / protocol funding orgs
Fund core contributors or infrastructure. Co-campaigns around major milestones — they fund, you amplify. Relationship-first, no cold pitches.

### Regional community orgs
Local chapters, meetup groups, regional builder communities. Supply-side for your content/programs and distribution partners. Start by featuring their work.

### Foundation / working groups
If your org has any lineage to a foundation or standards body, maintain and deepen this relationship — co-funding for regional programs, co-branding on education content.

## Contact Research Checklist (run before any Tier 1/2 outreach)

- [ ] Found the right contact (not just the CEO)
- [ ] Read their last 10 X/social posts — know their current narrative
- [ ] Checked GitHub for recent activity
- [ ] Identified one specific thing to reference in outreach
- [ ] Checked for a warm intro path
- [ ] Verified no existing relationship with your org already (check with team)
- [ ] Chosen the right channel (X DM / Discord / email / LinkedIn) based on org type
