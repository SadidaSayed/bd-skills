# web3-bd

A Claude skill: a general playbook for running business development (BD) at any Web3/crypto organization — protocols, DeFi platforms, exchanges, media, dev tooling, infrastructure, or product studios.

## What it covers

- **Web3/crypto BD cultural norms** — what works and what kills deals with crypto-native, builder-first communities
- **8-stage BD lifecycle** — identify → research → initiate → explore → propose → negotiate → close → renew
- **Pitching patterns** by target type (protocols, staking/infra, exchanges, hackathon platforms, institutional/TradFi entrants)
- **Pipeline management** — fields to track, review cadence
- **Content–BD intersection** — how content creates BD surface

## Files

- `SKILL.md` — the main playbook. Start here.
- `templates.md` — outreach message templates, proposal format, follow-up and renewal templates (generic placeholders — fill in your org/product specifics before sending)
- `targets.md` — BD target categories with pitch angles, contact strategies, and deal structures
- `crypto-context.md` — template for ecosystem fluency (macro signals, vocabulary, competitive positioning) — fill in current data per engagement

## How to use

This is generic by design. Before using it for a specific org, fill in Section 1 of `SKILL.md` ("Org & Product Context") with:
1. What the org is and what its products/BD surface actually are
2. Credibility anchors / positioning
3. Revenue model or deal-size context

Upload all five files in this repo (keep them together, flat, no subfolder) as a skill in Claude, or point Claude at this repo directly.
