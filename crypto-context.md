# Crypto/Web3 Ecosystem Context — BD Conversations Reference

A template for the kind of ecosystem fluency that anchors BD pitches, handles objections, and adds credibility to outreach. The specific numbers below are examples of the *categories* worth tracking — always verify current figures (search or ask the user) before citing them in a real conversation; crypto data goes stale fast.

---

## Macro signals worth tracking

| Signal | Why it matters for BD |
|--------|------------------------|
| Stablecoin TVL / supply on relevant chain(s) | TradFi/institutional pitch: "this is financial infrastructure, not a crypto experiment" |
| ETF or institutional capital inflows | Institutional pitch: real capital is here |
| L2/rollup or competing-chain TVL and share | Chain BD pitch: builders are the scarce resource, not capital |
| New chain/L2 launch cadence | Fragmentation = builder competition = your distribution value |
| Regional developer share (e.g. a country's share of new devs globally) | Regional pitch anchor — always cite a specific, current number |
| Token price performance vs. peers | Be ready to acknowledge if it comes up — shows you're not living in a bubble |

## Ecosystem-specific technical context (fill in per chain/protocol)

If your org operates in a specific ecosystem (Ethereum, Solana, Cosmos, etc.), maintain a running note of:
- **Client/validator diversity or decentralization risk**, if relevant — a concrete, sourced, live number is a strong BD hook ("the fix is distribution, not engineering — that's exactly what we can help with")
- **Upcoming protocol upgrades/hard forks** and their timeline — upgrade windows are forced-action BD moments (staking protocols need to educate operators, infra companies need to reach customers)
- **Major ecosystem debates or narratives** currently in play, so you don't accidentally contradict them in a pitch

---

## Regional / emerging market signals

Useful for regional pitches:
- Developer share by country/region (cite a specific, current, sourced number)
- Major recent events (conferences, hackathons) in that region and their scale
- Regulatory context if relevant (tax treatment, licensing) — note honestly, don't spin
- Named local platforms/communities and their reach, if you know them

## Crypto BD vocabulary — words that land vs. words that don't

### Use these
- "Network health" / "decentralization"
- "Builder access" / "builder distribution"
- "Ecosystem coordination"
- "Grassroots communities"
- "Trust-minimized" / "permissionless"
- "Open source"
- "Aligned" / "mission-aligned"

### Avoid these (in crypto-native BD contexts)
- "Go-to-market" (fine internally, sounds corporate externally)
- "Growth hacking"
- "Virality"
- "KPIs" / "OKRs" (use "success criteria" or "what good looks like")
- "Synergy"
- "ROI" (use "what this unlocks for you")
- "Impressions" / "reach" as the primary value proposition
- "Token incentives" unless they bring it up first
- "Community management" (implies broadcast, not participation)

## Credibility anchors — how to build your own list

For your org, identify:
1. **Lineage/pedigree** — any notable spin-out origin, backers, or founding team credibility
2. **Shared infrastructure or partnerships** with recognized, credible entities
3. **Real traction numbers** — orgs served, users reached, volume, etc. (only cite what's verifiable)
4. **Unique positioning** — what nobody else does in exactly the same way
5. **Funding/allocation activity** — if your org funds or grants, that's a credibility anchor, not just a reporting one
